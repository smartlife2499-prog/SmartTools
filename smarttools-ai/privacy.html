Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

SmartTools AI (“we”, “our”, or “the site”) provides free browser-based utility and AI tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

2. Information We Collect

Local only: Most tools process your files and text entirely in your browser. Uploaded PDFs, images, and typed content are not sent to our servers.

LocalStorage: We store preferences (theme), usage counters, and optional history in your browser’s localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you clear it.

AI requests: When you use AI-powered tools, the text you submit is sent to our secure Cloudflare Function, which forwards it to Google Gemini. We do not permanently store these prompts.

Analytics & ads: We may use Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These services may collect standard data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and cookies.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.

4. Data Retention

Browser localStorage data remains until you clear it. We do not retain copies of your uploaded files or AI prompts on our servers beyond the short time needed to process the request.

5. Children’s Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect changes.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the Contact page.
